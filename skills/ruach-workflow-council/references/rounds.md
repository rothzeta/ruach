# Closed rounds and retained state

Use this reference when preparing access, closing a round or resuming a council.

## Access before round one

Give each member the same question/version and declared evidence, plus its role and bounded assignment. Keep peer answers and synthesis inaccessible until closure. Disjoint output filenames in a shared readable checkout do not provide independence.

Inventory effective access through worktrees, parent/source directories, snapshots, caches, plugin/account resources and alternate aliases. Check both required input/resource access and exclusion of harmless unique peer-answer markers under the actual subject's permissions. Record routes, resource identities and observed controls. Prompt omission, an offline path manifest or a member's promise not to read peers does not prove exclusion. Failed or unsupported controls leave independence unverified; preserve the evidence rather than certify a pass.

## Close and exchange

A round closes only when every required member has an actual durable report and a recorded Coordinator disposition. Track member ID, assignment version, report identity, observed identity and handled disposition. Record missing, failed or blocked members explicitly; neither terminal state nor elapsed time supplies their answer. An authorized amendment can change roster or question, with its source and effect on remaining rounds retained.

Report corrections and submission retries are not new deliberation rounds. Advance the completed count only on closure. Later-round input manifests name actual closed reports/versions, question changes, any synthesis and new evidence. Do not expose still-open peer responses within a later round either.

Members report:

- position and evidence with source/build identity and limitations;
- assumptions and their authority;
- accepted and rejected peer arguments, attributed to report/proposition IDs with reasons;
- changes since their previous position, including newly discovered requirements;
- retained disagreement, supported minority concerns and resolving evidence or owner choice.

In round one there are no peer arguments to accept or reject. Never invent them. Preserve substantial arguments in original reports even if synthesis compresses them.

## Synthesis and resumption

Organize synthesis by proposition: evidence, authors' supporting/opposing reasons, accepted/rejected arguments and why, changed assumptions, unresolved objections, resolving evidence, recommendation and owner-adoption status. Different layouts are valid if this information stays attributable. Do not average unsupported claims or discard a supported objection because more members disagree.

On resume, reconcile the task record with retained reports and actual session liveness. Consume an observed but unhandled report once, recording disposition before dependent action. Reuse handled identities; a later evidence-only commit is not automatically a new answer. Restore completed/remaining counts and owner amendments rather than replaying round one. Unknown or missing decisions remain gaps. Preserve still-valid authority; a paused manual mode and an exhausted automatic mode retain their explicit continuation boundary.
