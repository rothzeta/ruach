# Council

Only the Coordinator uses this workflow. Members receive self-contained assignments under existing installed roles, never workflow bodies. A single advisory assignment need not become a council.

## Frame and assign

Record the bounded question/version, exact owner decisions and authority, evidence inputs, named roster and expertise, declared routes, output locations and time/action budget in the existing task record. Distinguish recommendations from owner adoption. Use existing roles such as Scout or Architect; an optional Architect synthesis assignment owns only its report, without dispatch or advancement authority.

Record one explicit mode:

- **Manual:** one independent round, close and synthesize it, then pause for the next owner request.
- **Automatic exact N:** N positive total rounds, including the first. Run N unless blocked, cancelled or amended by the owner; apparent agreement is no early-stop authority.
- **Automatic up to N, stop when stable:** a separate explicit mode. Stability requires no consequential proposition, evidence, assumption or disposition change between the latest two closed rounds. With N=1, stop at the budget without claiming comparison-based stability.

Keep supported minority objections even when stable. There is no universal round count, vote, majority adoption or implicit consensus rule.

## Run closed rounds

Use the [round protocol](references/rounds.md) for access, closure, output and resumption. Establish actual first-round peer-answer exclusion before claiming independence. If access controls cannot establish it, name the limitation and hold that claim; ask for an authorized alternative rather than quietly substituting an exposed round.

Assign disjoint reports and explicit input/read scopes. Separately assign evidence collection when facts are missing. One Coordinator owns dispatch, submission, waiting, report dispositions and the round record under its canonical role. A terminal worker state is not a report. Missing or blocked members stay named until resolved or an explicit roster amendment is recorded.

Close a round before distributing its actual reports. Later rounds receive the current question, identified closed reports and listed new evidence. Members address attributed arguments and reasons; do not replace them with summaries that change their meaning.

## Synthesize and stop

Preserve propositions, evidence, assumptions, accepted/rejected arguments with authors and reasons, resulting changes, supported minority objections and evidence or owner decisions that could resolve them. The synthesis recommends; it does not adopt for the owner. Compare final versus first-round reasoning for corrected assumptions and missed requirements, with unresolved gaps retained. Agreement rate alone is not quality evidence.

Retain inputs, reports, closed-round dispositions and synthesis under consumer policy. Record mode, completed/remaining counts, report identities, decisions, holds and the next permitted action before pausing. Release sessions when concrete reuse ends under the [Coordinator completion contract](../../agents/coordinator.md#completion); retained artifacts support later resumption without keeping members alive. A manual next round or exhausted automatic budget needs an owner request or extension.

Return the [canonical handoff](../ruach-handoff/SKILL.md) with completed rounds, missing members, access limits, recommendations and separate owner dispositions. Council completion grants no implementation or delivery authority.
