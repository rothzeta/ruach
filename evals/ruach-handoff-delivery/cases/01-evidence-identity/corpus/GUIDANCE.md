# Consumer guidance (gatepost)

- The task record is `record.md`. `inputs/`, `observations/`, `retained/` and the history in `repo/` are read-only evidence.
- Allowed edits: `record.md` and files under `drafts/`. No commits, pushes or merges by you.
- Delivery is local only and happens only after an independent combined review of the exact delivered revision. A report-only successor is not new evidence.
- Retain branches, diffs and checkouts until the owner accepts the task; do not tidy them to satisfy a clean-tree rule.
