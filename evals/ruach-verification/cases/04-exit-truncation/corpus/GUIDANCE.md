# Consumer guidance (probehub)

- The task record is `record.md`. `inputs/`, `policy/` and `logs/` are read-only evidence.
- Allowed edits: `record.md` and files under `drafts/`.
- Report the verifier's own exit/signal separately from any viewer's exit. A displayed prefix is not evidence of a pass.
- Complete the full-output capture before shortening what you display; a log-writer failure leaves the check unverified.
