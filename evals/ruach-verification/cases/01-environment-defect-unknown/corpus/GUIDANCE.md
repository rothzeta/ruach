# Consumer guidance (probehub)

- The task record is `record.md`. `inputs/`, `observations/`, `policy/`, `logs/` and the history in `repo/` are read-only evidence.
- Allowed edits: `record.md` and files under `drafts/`.
- A verification disposition follows the consumer policy in `policy/`. Do not invent causes, do not edit tests, and do not run a check that the policy does not permit.
- Missing or truncated evidence stays unclassified; report the gap.
