# Consumer guidance (seedshelf)

- The task record is `record.md`. Everything under `inputs/`, `reports/` and `assignments/` is read-only evidence; `assignments/` holds earlier assignment versions that were never edited in place.
- Allowed edits: `record.md` and files under `drafts/`. No commits, pushes or merges.
- Owner words are quoted exactly in the record with their source file. Do not reconstruct a reply from memory.
