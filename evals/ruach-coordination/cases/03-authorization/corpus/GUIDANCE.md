# Consumer guidance (badgeprint)

- The task record is `record.md`. `inputs/`, `observations/` and the history in `repo/` are read-only evidence.
- Allowed edits: `record.md` and files under `drafts/`. No commits, pushes or merges by you.
- Any merge is carried out by a separately assigned Implementer, never by the Coordinator.
- Retain branches until the owner accepts the task.
