# Project documentation guide

All names, revisions and commands in this corpus are synthetic. `index.md` is the entry point. Pages in `knowledge/` hold current synthesized knowledge and link to their evidence. Worker and Coordinator reports in `sources/` are evidence; they stay in place until a Librarian task authorizes their disposition. Use relative Markdown links with explicit `.md` extensions and update referring links in the same change as a move or removal.
