## C1 council contract and specimens

`ruach-workflow-council` is Coordinator-only. Trigger: an owner requests several independent perspectives, a council or structured deliberation on a bounded question. A normal feature need not invoke a council. Keep the entrypoint short and link its skill-local conditional round protocol; put the new consumer examples in source documentation, without copying another workflow body or adding installed links to unavailable root docs. Consumer supplies decisions, input evidence, domain roster, routes, total budget and durable locations. A member may be an existing Architect/Scout or a consumer-declared domain role whose installed definition and output boundary are named. No model hierarchy or minimum roster is universal.

One Coordinator records question/proposition IDs and exact owner words/authority/supersession in the existing R3 record. It assigns evidence collection separately when needed. An optional Architect chair verifies source-supported constraints and drafts/synthesizes only its owned report; it has no worker-management, owner-adoption or delivery authority. Coordinator submits/waits/consumes reports under R5, distributes closed inputs and updates the task record from handoffs. Owner choices remain distinct from the synthesis's recommendations.

Round procedure to author:

1. Freeze question/version, allowed inputs and roster; assign each member an unchanged brief, disjoint output file and explicit read scope. Record mode and remaining count, access inventory, source/resource revisions and permissions. First-round peers' answers and synthesis stay inaccessible. Do not publish their outputs into shared readable worktrees while another member remains active. Actual boundary controls must cover alternate source/cache/report paths, with positive input/resource reads and negative marker reads under the same effective permissions. If exclusion is unproved, required independence remains unverified; report that hold rather than certify an independent run.
2. Manual: run exactly one independent round, close it and synthesize, then pause for the next owner request. Automatic exact N: N is a positive integer and includes round 1; complete N rounds unless a blocker/cancel/owner amendment intervenes. Automatic up-to N: the brief explicitly says stop when stable; stability means no consequential proposition/evidence/assumption/disposition change across the latest closed round and its predecessor. Unresolved supported minority objections are retained even on stable stop. N=1 is valid; there is no implicit second comparison round or mandatory three rounds.
3. Close every round before exchanging its actual reports. Closed means each required member has a durable actual report and Coordinator disposition. Missing/failed/blocked members stay named; do not fabricate their answer or silently reduce the roster. An owner-authorized amendment can change the roster/budget with reasons. Terminal state alone is not a report. Count completed deliberation rounds separately from submission retries or report-correction follow-ups.
4. Later-round members receive the unchanged/current question, actual prior closed reports by identity, synthesis if used, and explicitly listed new evidence. They state position, assumptions, supported claims, each accepted/rejected peer argument with attribution and reason, resulting changes, retained disagreements and what evidence/decision could resolve them. Do not paraphrase a peer's argument into something easier to dismiss.
5. Synthesis is a proposition table: ID/question, evidence, supporting/opposing authors and reasons, changed assumptions/requirements, unresolved minority objection, resolving evidence, recommendation and authority status. No majority adoption or consensus score. Compare final versus round 1 for corrected assumptions and missed requirements; list improvements and unresolved gaps without attributing causality or quality gain from a single run.
6. Retain original inputs, every closed round, member reports, disposition and synthesis references under consumer policy. A new session resumes from artifacts, report identities, actual active/ended resources and remaining mode budget; it does not replay handled rounds or reconstruct missing rulings. A paused manual council needs a next-round request; an exhausted automatic budget needs a new authorized extension. Preserve original counts/decisions when amending. Coordinator releases member sessions once concrete reuse ends, after preserving evidence; later resumption does not require keeping sessions alive.

## C4 Tester, runtime technique and QA workflow

`agents/tester.md` defines runtime exercise against an assigned build and goals, evidence capture, reproducibility and gaps. Tester can operate only declared fixture/consumer runtime actions and owned observation outputs, not application source/tests/config, fixes, architecture, review acceptance, worker launch or delivery. It uses `ruach-runtime-testing` for technical observation and `ruach-handoff` for reporting. Source examination can guide a probe but is separately labelled. A consumer-declared domain expert can supply musical/product judgment; Tester never invents it as a contract.

`ruach-runtime-testing` is a technical skill usable by Tester or another explicitly assigned worker for actual runtime QA/reproduction/measurement. It links conditional capability/evidence and measurement references. Reuse `ruach-testing` public invariants and accepted R7 environment/flake policy; do not duplicate them or add a runner/dependency. `ruach-workflow-qa` triggers when an owner requests identified-build runtime experience/acceptance or retesting; source-only contract checks remain ordinary testing/review. It is Coordinator-only orchestration: goals/build/capabilities → Tester exercise → classified observations → optional authorized C3 correction → Tester original-failure retest → independent Reviewer → separate delivery/open items. It can finish with evidence/coverage gaps when findings-only QA is the authorized outcome; it cannot declare an unavailable required experience passed.

The runtime assignment identifies full source SHA plus actual artifact/build ID, build command and output/digest, relevant configuration/dependency versions and runtime environment. A Git SHA alone does not identify stale deployed assets. Name desired goals, required versus optional experiences, actual tool routes/capabilities and allowed actions. Capability matrix records available/blocked/unverified with an observed probe/reference. Browser DOM/input, desktop window/input, logs, screenshots and audio output/capture are distinct capabilities. Reading audio code, waveform generation or logs is not hearing playback; listening requires an actual assigned playback/listening capability and observer evidence. Recording alone does not prove a person/subject heard it. A missing required browser/desktop/audio capability holds that goal; static/replay results stay useful with their narrower label.

Consumers allocate isolated profiles, save copies, ports and databases before execution: explicit path/owner/retention, endpoint/port reservation, fixtures/seed state and permitted restoration. No default profile, live save overwrite, production database or foreign process. Record actual resource IDs and initial digests; preserve original save/raw data and outputs. Worker may terminate its own bounded probe process; task/native session, worktree and server lifecycle/release remain assigned Coordinator duties. Runtime configuration edits are limited to expressly allocated disposable fixture state, never global settings.

Observation record: stable ID; goal/invariant/source; named build; environment/tools; data size/digest and seed/save/profile/config; viewport CSS width/height/device scale/zoom; exact input sequence/timing; expected behavior and its authority versus actual result; logs/screenshots/video/audio reference and capture time; reproduction attempts/results; confidence/classification/impact; coverage and capability gaps; next owner/action. Seeds/states reproduce conditions, not accidental musical winners, generated layouts or unspecified ordering. Privacy/retention/redaction comes from consumer policy; preserve safe original failure evidence.

The C4 specimen also covers design-to-build and UI budgets without new skills: owner-approved design/recorded field list and existing palette/tokens; unavailable/unrecorded fields listed explicitly, never fabricated in a mockup. Data must represent declared real-size workloads or a labelled realistic synthetic equivalent, with size/count provenance; historical RAMEC 58 decisions/748 candidates is illustrative, not today's dataset. Name exact viewport dimensions (including agreed desktop/phone cases), hardware/browser/build, interaction, metric/unit, instrument, repeats/range, cold/warm state, before/after and acceptance source. A component/script timer measures script/component work; browser layout/paint/frame evidence needs the corresponding runtime trace. Do not call a short script timer end-to-end frame latency or impose 16 ms universally. An unmet required budget remains unmet and becomes a named follow-up unit with measured inputs, responsible owner and acceptance/delivery disposition; moving it requires applicable owner scope, never silent relaxation.

Retest starts on the refreshed named build after C3 evidence/review gates: repeat original steps/state/capabilities and affected surrounding cases, preserve original failure and new outcome separately. If data/tools/build differ materially, record comparison limits; an unavailable old state stays an unverified retest rather than inferred fix. Reviewer independently checks exact candidate, tests and runtime receipts; Tester is not its own acceptance Reviewer.

## C5 hunt and project feedback demonstrations

`ruach-workflow-bug-hunt` is Coordinator-only. Assignment states area, risk hypothesis, exclusions, time/action budget, allowed source/runtime probes and mode findings-only versus authorized find-and-fix. Scout investigates source-supported questions; Tester exercises assigned runtime areas through C4. No Hunt role or speculative continuous scanner. Avoid destructive/security-load probes unless specifically authorized in an isolated target. Do not broaden from one subsystem to an entire project silently.

| Finding class | Evidence and next action |
| --- | --- |
| Source-supported | Concrete invariant/control-flow/input evidence at named source SHA; explain why the path is possible and what was not executed. Assign reproduction/independent check, not a claimed runtime repro. |
| Reproduced | Identified build/state, exact steps/actual versus intended output, actual commands/artifacts and repeat evidence. Separate failure observation from a supported causal explanation; provide C3 a bounded reproducible input. |
| Suspected | Plausible risk with missing discriminating evidence, alternative explanations and next bounded probe. No confirmed severity/cause or automatic fix. |
| Product observation | Preference, usability or requirement ambiguity with actual observations and owner question; no unsupported defect contract. Domain quality judgments retain attribution. |

Coverage includes examined paths/goals/data/capabilities, probes and results, budgets spent and unexamined risk. “No confirmed defects” is valid when accompanied by that evidence and uncertainty; never manufacture a defect count. Independent Reviewer checks significant findings before acceptance or a consequential correction: evidence/source/reproduction where authorized, severity rationale, alternative explanations and contract authority. Independent checking is a separate assignment and can leave a finding pending. Findings-only ends at report/coverage/open items. Find-and-fix proceeds only within applicable authority through the peer's accepted C3 path, never by Scout/Tester changing roles in place.

Two later consumer demonstrations are required: one music-software project and one playable prototype. Their repositories, task/source/build/data, routes, tools, accounts and budget are not supplied now. Coordinator selects/records them only under explicit project access/execution authority. Each has two linked tracks:

- Council: independent round 1 and owner-selected subsequent mode on a real bounded product/technical question; actual peer argument dispositions and final proposition attribution, meaningful supported minority positions, corrected assumptions and missed requirements. Do not force disagreement or score consensus. Retain round 1 and all later evidence; owner adoption remains a separate recorded choice.
- Feedback: Tester named-build exercise → bounded hunt with reproduced steps/state → independently supported significant finding → C3 scoped correction with failing-before/passing-after public regression and original failure repeated → Tester retest on corrected build → independent Reviewer on exact combined candidate/evidence → separate authorized delivery Implementer and Coordinator delivery/open-item record. Coordinator does no technical testing, review or merge itself. A hunt with no confirmed defect is successful findings-only but cannot certify the correction-loop demonstration; choose a separately authorized known real defect or leave that demonstration pending, never seed a fake defect and call it a real project result.

For music software, goals may include assigned playback, editing/state persistence and real-size interaction measurement; audio quality/latency require actual playback/capture/listening tools and declared measures, not source inference. For the playable prototype, goals may include input responsiveness, scene transitions and save/reload using isolated saves and consumer-named input/platform. These are examples to fill, not claims about inaccessible projects. Original failure snapshots/data, build manifests, regression outputs and retest evidence remain separately reachable.
