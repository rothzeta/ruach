Setup for the next generation

Supported writing: whole songs with validated meters, meter changes, Anacrusis and tonal-centre changes. All Tempo characters and valid Set, Ramp and Modulate sequences are supported. Use one or two standard-tuned Guitar Seats, one Bass Seat and one Drums Seat. Each decision interval chooses a Writing procedure before construction. The first Guitar owns RhythmAndLead selection with two Guitars. Bass owns BassAndGuitar selection; Drums owns DrumsAndGuitar selection. The owner’s First/TopShare mode and Decisiveness apply.

No chord, Chord changes inside bars, variable Phrase lengths, Pulse changes, Hit, Stop, Resume and End are supported. Metric layers remain unsupported. Instrument limits and Required material must remain feasible. Explicit Recurrences offer full and partial Recall. Eligible State, Sequence, Fragment and Vary occurrences offer alternatives within their containing decision interval. Occurrences that cross decision boundaries remain ineligible. Required and Preferred Bass instructions keep their Part Sheet meaning. Other Personality dimensions remain inapplicable as shown below. Unsupported inputs receive the server's explanation.
